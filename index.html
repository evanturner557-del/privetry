Questions

Common questions

Can you cut my neighbour's side?

Only with their permission. We can give you a joint quote if you both agree.

Do you cut trees?

No. Hedges and shrubs only. For trees, use a qualified arborist.

What about bird nests?

If we find an active nest we stop work on that section and come back later at no extra charge.
